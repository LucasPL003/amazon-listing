# Image Similarity Boundaries

Use this reference before writing prompts or generating images from competitor references.

## Allowed Similarity

It is acceptable to learn from:

- General image role, such as main image, detail close-up, scene image, comparison chart, bundle layout
- Broad composition logic, such as product centered, diagonal arrangement, callouts around details, before/after split
- Information hierarchy, such as hero benefit first, specs second, usage scene third
- Category conventions, such as clean white main image, lifestyle scene, macro detail, size comparison
- General mood, such as premium, minimalist, cute, technical, family-friendly, outdoor, giftable

## Not Allowed

Do not copy or recreate:

- Competitor product photos, exact backgrounds, exact props, exact camera angle plus layout combination
- Brand names, logos, trademarks, packaging artwork, watermarks, badges, seals, or model names
- Identifiable faces, people, rooms, illustrations, charts, icons, mascots, or graphic systems from the competitor
- Exact Japanese copy, diagrams, comparison tables, annotation positions, color blocks, or layout templates
- Claims, certifications, awards, rankings, review counts, or specs that the user's product does not support

## Safe Transformation Rules

Change several dimensions at once:

- Use the user's product assets, not the competitor's product image
- Change background, color palette, props, crop, camera angle, and annotation structure
- Reword benefits based on the user's product facts
- Replace competitor-specific packaging or labels with the user's own materials, or omit them
- Use category-standard icons only when they are generic and newly created

If the user asks for an overly close copy, refuse that part briefly and offer a safer alternative: keep the same selling goal but change the visual system and wording.

## Prompt Safety Checklist

Before generation, confirm:

- The prompt references the user's product, not the competitor product
- The prompt does not request a copied layout or brand assets
- Any text claims are supported by user-provided product facts
- The image role is clear and useful for Amazon.co.jp shoppers
- Main image remains product-focused and not visually cluttered
